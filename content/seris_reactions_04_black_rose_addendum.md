# Volume IV — The Black Rose Addendum

The silver book would not open.

Seris examined the clasp, tested it for ordinary wards and found a single line engraved beneath the hinge.

## Design Note

Emily's first paragraph was almost apologetic.

The Black Rose settings had emerged during what she called a *particularly severe period of productive research*. They were dormant layers hidden within her artifacts: not new personalities, not possessing spirits, but alternate rules intended for users who wanted the machinery to become colder, more humiliating and less sympathetic to the dignity they carried into a session.

Activation required the user to speak a complete phrase voluntarily:

*C.: I objected to this password.*

*E.: It is unambiguous.*

The book, mercifully, made no comment.

## The Whore-Maker

Black Rose did not give the Whore-Maker a headmistress's voice. Emily regarded that as theatrical clutter.

## The Beneficent Jewelry

The jewelry stayed silent.

That silence distinguished it from the Whore-Maker. As arousal rose, temporary inscriptions appeared across the wearer's skin in elegant pink script. *C.: It rejected “technically responsive adult with situationally elevated desire.”*

No inscription appeared.

She was almost disappointed.

## The Penitent Coil

In Black Rose mode, the Coil converted heroic independence into a service problem.

It arranged the volunteer so that one part of her restraint could be released only by bringing another consenting woman to orgasm through sufficiently competent attention. A loosened wrist might require skilled use of the mouth. Relief for a strained posture might depend upon patient work with bound hands. The final knot would remain closed until the recipient confirmed not merely climax but care afterward.

The Coil did not permit a recipient to be used as a key without consent. It asked first. It also prevented her from falsely declaring satisfaction to shorten the volunteer's ordeal.

*Z.: That last function is insulting.*

*C.: You tried it.*

*Z.: To test the safeguard.*

*E.: Naturally.*

The setting attacked self-sufficiency by making freedom depend upon service. Strength could not pull the knots apart. Sorcery could not circumvent them. The woman who had entered voluntarily had to turn her attention outward, study another person's pleasure and earn each increment of her own mobility through competence.

Humiliation sharpened the service without cheapening it. The Coil might tighten when the volunteer became careless, reposition her when she treated the recipient's pleasure as a task to rush, or display an unflattering measure of how much more aroused the bound server had become than the woman she was meant to satisfy.

That last comparison produced five pages of argument.

Emily had preserved all five.

## The Appraising Glass

The Glass altered nothing physical. It merely removed the ability to pretend.

Its Black Rose captions selected the angles the subject most wanted hidden and described observable facts with merciless economy.

**SUBJECT'S RESISTANCE HAS BECOME DECORATIVE.**

**SUBJECT IS WATCHING HERSELF SUBMIT.**

**SUBJECT'S BODY RESPONDS POSITIVELY TO THIS CAPTION.**

If the subject closed her eyes, the Glass waited. If she turned her face away, it displayed the angle to which she was turning. If she insisted the posture was not affecting her, it placed the earlier and later images beside one another and marked every difference: flush, parted lips, flexed toes, softened belly, dampness, the rope's increasingly visible pressure against a body no longer holding itself rigid.

*Z.: The captions should not be allowed to comment upon the captions.*

*A.G.: SUBJECT OBJECTS TO RECURSION BECAUSE RECURSION WAS EFFECTIVE.*

*Z.: Emily, why does the mirror have archival ink?*

## The Resonant Pearl

Black Rose transformed the Pearl's ordinary wagers into decisions made under desperation.

The probability of immediate release was always real. Emily prohibited false choices. Yet the odds worsened as the potential reward became more desirable, and most failures imposed one or more consequences: stronger stimulation, additional time, a more exposing position, or a new wager that could be declined only by accepting a written record of surrender.

The Pearl displayed no percentages once the session began unless the user had specifically requested them beforehand. Instead it offered covered runes.

**ONE GRANTS RELEASE. THREE ADD AN HOUR. CHOOSE.**

The woman chose.

If she lost, the Pearl revealed all four, proving that mercy had existed and she had missed it by her own hand.

If she refused to choose, the default consequence was milder but more humiliating. The Glass displayed a receipt:

**SUBJECT PREFERRED CERTAIN DENIAL TO THE RISK OF EARNING RELEASE.**

Zephira considered this worse than losing.

Cordelia considered it fascinating until she was the one making the choice.

*C.: Probabilities feel different when one is already desperate.*

*Z.: This is why casinos serve alcohol.*

*E.: And why my Pearl does not.*

The setting attacked judgment without removing it. The player remained responsible for every gamble. A calm limit prevented catastrophic escalation. Within that limit, however, desperation was allowed to make a fool of her.

Seris read every example.

She correctly solved the optimal choice in each case and knew with growing discomfort that optimality might not survive the Pearl warming against her skin.

## Courteous Silence

The gag's severe setting did not begin its timer when inserted.

It waited for an undignified sound.

A controlled breath did not count. A deliberate theatrical moan did not count. The artifact listened for the involuntary noise produced when composure broke: a needy whimper, a muffled cry, the broken little sound of surprise when stimulation struck a place the wearer had tried not to expose.

Once it heard one, the timer began.

If the wearer recovered perfect silence, the program adjusted until it drew another. It did not seek pain or panic. It sought the voice beneath discipline and treated every failed attempt at dignity as useful calibration.

Zephira proved especially vulnerable because she regarded vocal control as a matter of pride. Cordelia proved especially vulnerable because she tried to turn every failure into a joke and discovered laughter could dissolve into helpless sound halfway through.

**COURTESY THRESHOLD MET,** the inset would announce in small blue letters. **TIMER COMMENCING.**

*Z.: I dislike this artifact.*

*C.: You packed it for the monastery.*

*Z.: I dislike forgetting it.*

## The Mutable Instrument

The Instrument remained cheerful.

That was worse.

Its ordinary personality resembled an eager saleswoman determined to ensure no sensitive area went neglected. Black Rose removed none of the brightness. It merely changed the standard from *pleased* to *complete*.

**Attention discrepancy detected!** it would announce. **Two areas remain insufficiently stimulated. Correcting now!**

It treated the volunteer as an incomplete service assignment. Every neglected opening, every untouched breast, every pair of curling toes, every region left without pressure or friction became a deficiency to fill. If one implement withdrew, another took its place. If the wearer attempted to protect one sensitive point by shifting her hips, the Instrument happily identified the newly exposed alternative.

It was too bubbly for its methods and too methodical for mercy.

The volunteer could set hard boundaries before activation. Within them, the Instrument interpreted *don't neglect anything* with the zeal of a craftswoman offended by unfinished work.

*C.: It once apologized to my left foot.*

*Z.: Why?*

*E.: Forty-three seconds below the right foot's stimulation total.*

*Z.: Monstrous.*

*E.: Quality control.*

## The Complete Curriculum

Cordelia and Zephira eventually returned to the Whore-Maker with every compatible Black Rose setting active.

Emily did not retell the session. She presented the interaction map.

The jewelry supplied inscriptions and denied release until each woman admitted them. The Coil made partial freedom conditional upon competent service. The Glass displayed what pride wished hidden. The Pearl offered bad bargains at the moments judgment became weakest. Courteous Silence measured every involuntary sound. The Mutable Instrument corrected whatever the larger machine overlooked. The Whore-Maker coordinated the whole with dispassionate instructions and addressed both volunteers by the role they had requested.

The system escalated because each artifact used the others' results. A lost Pearl wager gave the Coil a more exposing arrangement. The new posture gave the Glass a better angle. Its caption provoked the sound that started Courteous Silence. The jewelry wrote **HORNY SLUT** across the woman who objected most loudly. The Whore-Maker graded her objection as insufficient vocal gratitude and ordered repetition.

Neither woman could preserve the fiction that the machinery was doing something to an unwilling self. They had spoken the password. They had chosen the limits. Every humiliating consequence was evidence that the earlier, composed woman had known exactly what the later, desperate woman would want—and had arranged not to believe her complaints.

Emily's conclusion was unexpectedly restrained.

**Black Rose does not reveal a lower or truer identity. It provides a temporary grammar in which pride, service, humiliation and desire may contradict one another without requiring resolution. The volunteer remains herself. She has merely requested that the artifacts stop being polite about what her body enjoys.**

Three annotations followed.

*C.: This is annoyingly good.*

*Z.: Do not become sentimental.*

*E.: Further severe settings are theoretically possible.*

*C.: Do not include that sentence.*

*Z.: Leave it.*

The silver volume ended.

Seris sat very still.

She had read Cain to learn what the Beneficent Attire did. She had read Emily to learn why someone would ask an artifact to do worse. The answer was neither corruption nor recklessness. It was authorship. Cordelia and Zephira designed the terms while calm, then took pleasure in becoming women who could no longer negotiate with their own earlier decisions.

The humiliation was real because their pride was real.

The helplessness was real because the locks were real.

The consent did not make either sensation imaginary. It gave them a shape that could be entered and left without destroying the women who enjoyed them.

Seris closed the clasp.

When she stacked the four volumes, she discovered a fifth space in the fitted case. It contained no book, only a thin slate and a silver stylus.

Letters appeared across the slate.

**SUGGESTED NEXT INQUIRY: PRACTICAL RECONSTRUCTION.**

Seris held the stylus for a long time.

Then she wrote beneath the suggestion:

**Eventually.**

The slate considered her answer.

**INADEQUATE SPECIFICITY.**

Seris smiled, shut the case, and took the stylus home.
